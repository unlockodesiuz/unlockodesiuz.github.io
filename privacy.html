Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Peer Talk Content Engine ("the app") is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Peer Talk channel to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram). It is not available to other users.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (display name, account identifier), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account.

What the app stores

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos. No personal data of any other person is collected. Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Data from the platforms

The app reads public performance figures (views, likes) of the owner's own videos to decide what to make next. It does not read other people's data.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's app settings. Locally stored tokens are deleted on request by the owner.
